# Development Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation
Capture requirements, establish cross-platform structure and define the prototype design system.

## Phase 1 — Prototype 0.1
Build Home and navigation; Learn & Practice preview; Solo/Casual/Competitive entry flows; Friends, Favorite Partners, Recent Players and invitations; profiles and partnership stats; competitive rank explanations; live customization; an interactive four-player table with both team scores; and simulated chat/Quick Chat/translation/mute/report behavior.

**Exit criterion:** the core interface feels understandable and enjoyable on a real Android device.

## Phase 2 — Canasta engine
Formalize rulesets, deterministic card/deck model, scoring, legal-action validation, meld/discard/frozen-pile/go-out logic, tests and seat-limited computer-player interfaces.

## Phase 3 — Accounts and social backend
Authentication, profiles, friends, favorite partners, blocks, presence, invitations, private tables, statistics, achievements, match history, notifications and privacy.

## Phase 4 — Realtime multiplayer
Server-authoritative matches, matchmaking, reconnection/inactivity handling, competitive rating/divisions, match logs, anti-cheat telemetry, chat and translation.

## Phase 5 — Moderation and trust
Reports, sportsmanship feedback, profile/avatar moderation, abuse controls and competitive abandonment policy.

## Phase 6 — Beta
Android testing, iOS TestFlight, accessibility, performance/device coverage and tuning from real play data.

## Phase 7 — Store launch
Final branding review, privacy/terms/store disclosures, production signing, Google Play and Apple App Store submissions, monitoring and staged rollout.

## Guiding constraint
Do not build expensive production backend systems until Prototype 0.1 validates the interaction and visual direction.
