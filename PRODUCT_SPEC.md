# Endra Canasta Product Specification

Working product specification for Prototype 0.1.

## Product pillars

- Learnability: teach complete beginners through guided play.
- Social connection: useful friends, favorite partners, recent players, invitations, and shared history.
- Fair play: server-authoritative production multiplayer; hidden hands are never exposed to another seat.
- Personalization: local table, cards, profile, interface, sound, and accessibility customization without gameplay advantage.

## Play areas

1. Learn & Practice
2. Solo Play
3. Casual Play
4. Competitive Play
5. Friends & Private Tables

Supporting areas include Profile, Friends, Messages, Statistics, Achievements, Match History, Customize, and Settings.

## Prototype 0.1

Validate the primary navigation, learning preview, social flows, competitive presentation, live customization, and an interactive four-player table on a real Android device. Simulated players/data are acceptable in this phase, but visible controls should respond.

## Production principles

Account Level, Competitive Division, and Competitive Rating remain separate. Competitive matchmaking starts narrow and asks before expanding. Friends can intentionally queue as partners. Competitive matches provide no private partner chat.

Production game state is server-authoritative. The server owns shuffle, deck, scoring, and legal-action validation. Each client receives only information legally visible to its seat.

Customization includes profile/avatar, frames, table colors/themes, card faces and backs, interface accents, sounds, effects, and accessibility. Cosmetics never change game information or confer advantage.

The eventual commercial product targets Android and iOS. Final branding, store identifiers, backend hosting, legal documents, and monetization remain deferred until later milestones.
